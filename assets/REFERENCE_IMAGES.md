# Internal generation references

BrewAI can use neutral internal reference images in addition to the customer's
product photo. These files guide geometry only; prompts explicitly prevent their
background, lighting, logos, and text from leaking into the result.

## Storage layout

- `generated-images/bottle-references/<flaschenTyp>.png`
- `generated-images/glass-references/<glasTyp>.png`
- `assets/campaign-references/*`, `assets/reportage-references/*`, `assets/premium-references/*` (bundled look libraries, see below)

Local development uses the same filenames below `assets/bottle-references/` and
`assets/glass-references/` as a fallback. Silhouette PNGs for every glass type
live in `assets/glass-references/` (regenerate via `node scripts/generate-glass-references.mjs`).

Supported glass filenames are:

- `pils_tulpe.png`
- `weizen.png`
- `willibecher.png`
- `masskrug.png`
- `ipa_teku.png`
- `schwenker.png`
- `stange.png`

Use one neutral, high-resolution photograph per shape: centered three-quarter
view, plain background, no branding, no props, no dramatic light, and the full
silhouette visible. Product photos remain the authority for the actual bottle or
can and label. Look and scene references should continue to use the existing
per-generation upload fields.

## Look libraries (reportage, premium, campaign)

All three photo styles share one loader: `src/lib/openai/styleLookReferences.ts`.
Each bundled look image is registered in `LOOK_LIBRARY` with two tags:

- `container`: `bottle`, `can` or `any` — a bottle brand never gets a can-only
  look (cans pull the model toward a can), cans prefer can looks.
- `light`: `day`, `night` or `any` — a night flash look never steers a daylight
  motif. Night means `tageszeit` `nacht`, `blaue_stunde` or `abend_warm`.

Up to two looks are attached per image, blurred before upload so only light,
crop and energy remain. Each entry sets its own `blur`: third-party photos use
the strong default (22), own AI-generated looks with invented people and blank
labels use `LIGHT_LOOK_BLUR` (4) so flash, room and crop stay readable. At that
light blur the model still copied the same faces into every customer image, so
each light-blurred entry lists its `faces` (percent boxes `[left, top, right,
bottom]`); those regions get a feathered, strong blur (`FACE_BLUR`). If no look matches the time of day, a look with
another light is sent instead of none. Every variant of one job gets a rotated selection so
the variants do not look alike. Prompts forbid copying people, packages,
logos or lettering; the customer's product photo stays authoritative.

Style definitions (see `buildPhotoStyleLockFragment`):

- `reportage` — candid flash snapshot: Stammtisch, beer garden, fest, keg
  tapping, brewhouse. On-camera direct flash (fill flash by day), imperfect
  crop, product casually part of the moment.
- `premium` — quiet product photography in a real place: product sharp and
  readable, 85mm, people at most secondary.
- `campaign` — art-directed key visual, product dominates. In feed/social mode
  the product stays in the lower 62% so the headline has room.

### Adding look images

1. Use only own or licensed photos — no third-party ads, no recognizable
   celebrities, no readable foreign brands. Keep the source sharp; blurring
   happens in code (`blur` per entry). AI-generated looks: blank labels,
   varied ages and faces, no smoking.
2. Put the file into `assets/<style>-references/`.
3. Register it in `LOOK_LIBRARY` with `container` and `light` — and with
   `faces` for every visible face when it uses `LIGHT_LOOK_BLUR`.
4. `styleLookReferences.test.ts` checks that every registered file exists.

Wanted next: more daylight reportage looks (beer garden at noon, courtyard
fest with fill flash), premium looks with bottles in Wirtshaus/cellar settings,
and campaign looks with German bottle types (NRW, swing-top, Steinie).

## Deployment

Every folder read via `process.cwd()/assets` must be listed in
`outputFileTracingIncludes` in `next.config.ts`; otherwise the live app runs
without those references. Glass silhouettes in `assets/glass-references/`
(generated by `scripts/generate-glass-references.mjs`) win over copies in Storage.
